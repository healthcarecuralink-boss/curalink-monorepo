Common questions

Home healthcare in Hyderabad & India, answered.

How much does home healthcare cost?

Pricing depends on the service — our lab tests start from ₹299, IV therapy from ₹499, doctor consultations from ₹799, and elderly day care from ₹1,499. Join early access for full pricing in your city.

How is Curalink different from Apollo HomeCare, Practo or Portea?

Like other home healthcare providers in India, Curalink sends verified nurses and doctors to your door — but pairs every visit with a calm, organized family health dashboard, insurance-ready invoicing, and transparent pricing shown upfront, not after you call.

Are your nurses and doctors verified?

Every professional is background-checked and license-verified before they ever reach your home — the same standard families expect from Apollo, Portea or HealthCare atHOME.

Which cities does Curalink serve?

We're launching first across Hyderabad — Banjara Hills, Jubilee Hills, Gachibowli, Madhapur, Kondapur, Secunderabad and beyond — with more of India to follow. See Hyderabad coverage →

Do you offer insurance-ready invoices?

Yes — every visit generates an itemized, insurance-ready invoice automatically, so reimbursement claims are simple.

I'm a nurse, doctor or care professional — can I join?

Yes — Curalink's partner network, CuralinkPlus, is free to join for nurses, doctors, vet nurses, pharmacies and ambulance operators.
