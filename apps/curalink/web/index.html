Common questions

Home healthcare in Hyderabad & India, answered.

How much does home healthcare cost?

A single visit starts from ₹599, travel included — injections, IV drips, dressings, catheter care and physio. Recovery courses are fixed-price: Recover-14 ₹6,999, Recover-7 ₹3,999, an IV antibiotic course ₹5,990. The final price is confirmed on WhatsApp before the nurse leaves.

How is Curalink different from Apollo HomeCare, Practo or Portea?

Curalink sends a verified, named nurse to your door on your doctor's written prescription, with OTP verification at the start and end of every visit, notes and wound photos on WhatsApp, and one itemised, claim-ready bill — not a marketplace of unnamed providers.

Are your nurses and doctors verified?

Every nurse is checked against the Telangana Nurses and Midwives Council register and police-verified before their first visit, and trained on Curalink's SOPs.

Which cities does Curalink serve?

We're launching first across Hyderabad — Banjara Hills, Jubilee Hills, Gachibowli, Madhapur, Kondapur, Secunderabad and beyond — with more of India to follow. See Hyderabad coverage →

Do I need a prescription?

For injections, IV drips and medicines, yes. Dressings, vitals and physio can be booked directly.

I'm a nurse, doctor or care professional — can I join?

Yes — Curalink's partner network, CuralinkPlus, is free to join for nurses, physiotherapists, doctors, pharmacies, nursing agencies and ambulance operators.
