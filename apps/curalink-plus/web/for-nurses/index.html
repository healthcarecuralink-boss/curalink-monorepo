How it works

Verified before your first visit.

  • Apply on WhatsApp, or with the form below
  • We check the Telangana Nurses and Midwives Council register and do police verification
  • Complete the 3-day induction and the skills test on day 3
  • Start and end each visit with the family's OTP, record notes in the app, and get paid
  • Through an agency already? Your agency can partner and share our bookings with you
