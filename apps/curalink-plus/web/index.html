Our Partners

A trusted partner to those who care

Nursing partners and doctors are the most trusted names in home healthcare. CuralinkPlus is built to support them first.

👩‍⚕️

Nurses & Physiotherapists

On-demand and booked visits grouped by area, 8 AM–10 PM. Signed SOPs, a clinical lead to call, and medicines that meet you — no pharmacy run.

  • Bookings near you, pick your hours
  • OTP check-in, notes & photos in-app
  • 3-day SOP induction
  • Paid on agreed terms, by bank transfer
🩺

Doctors

A paid medical director role — signs SOPs, checks prescriptions, reviews red flags — plus a video review panel for recovery-course patients. Paid roles are separate from referrals; referring a patient never earns anything (NMC 8.1).

  • Medical director — part-time, TGMC-registered
  • Video review panel, paid per review
  • Your name never used in our promotion
  • No fee, share or gift for a referral
💊

Pharmacies

Prescription orders from families booking nurse visits and recovery courses, sent to you. Deliver with the nurse or to the door — families pay MRP, itemised.

  • Orders arrive in the partner app
  • Rx only, checked at the bedside
  • Commission agreed in writing
  • No nurses of your own? We supply them
🚑

Ambulance Operators

Planned, non-emergency rides only — discharge day, and reviews back to the OPD. Emergencies always go to 108.

  • Your rate, shown to the family upfront
  • Pickup, drop & mobility needs sent ahead
  • Nurse escort added on request
  • Commission agreed in writing
🏥

Nursing & Physio Agencies

More home visits for your nurses, on Curalink SOPs. We bring the bookings, the app, and the pharmacy and ride partners you don't have.

  • On-demand + booked work, by area
  • Weekly scorecard per nurse
  • Our 3-day induction for your staff
  • Clinical backup, 8 AM–10 PM
🐾

Vet Care Team

Trusted home visits for pets — injections, wound care, medication and post-surgery support, delivered with the same SOPs and dignity.

  • Pet case profiles & scheduling
  • Home injections & wound care
  • Same live tracking & payouts
  • Commission agreed in writing

Common questions

Home healthcare operations, answered.

Does CuralinkPlus charge partners anything?

No sign-up fee. Curalink takes a commission on every order it brings you, including medicines, at a rate agreed in writing before you start — families never pay above the listed price or MRP.

Who can join as a partner in Hyderabad?

Nurses and physiotherapists, doctors, licensed pharmacies, ambulance operators and nursing or physiotherapy agencies — plus a vet care team for pet visits.

How is CuralinkPlus different from a job board?

Bookings come with signed SOPs, a clinical lead to call, and medicines that meet you from a partner pharmacy — whether you're a solo nurse or an agency.

How fast is onboarding?

Apply on WhatsApp. Nurses and agencies complete a 3-day SOP induction; pharmacies and ambulance partners start once licence and terms are signed.
